NirantharSeva · design system v1
Plain structure, rounded labels
Direction: the clinical skeleton from 1a — white sheet, hairline rules, dense rows, monospace numbers, one blue accent — with the rounded pill labels from 1b. Device fonts only, no images, no animation. Every value below is fixed; screens from here on use only these.
Colours are authored in oklch; the hex beside each is the fallback to emit for old Android WebViews.

4 · The three-shape rule
In sunlight, colour is the first thing to go. So the group a referral belongs to is carried by pill shape first, colour second, and a word always. Three shapes, no exceptions:
On the way
Fine — filled soft
Pale tinted fill, no border, dark text. Moving normally. Nothing for the worker to do today.
!Overdue
Overdue — solid dark
Strong solid fill, white bold text, and a round ! mark. The row also gets a 4px bar down its left edge. Reads as the heaviest thing on the screen even in greyscale.
✓Care finished
Done — outline only
No fill, 1.5px border, a ✓ (or ✕ for Lost). Empty shape means finished. The whole row also drops to normal weight and ink-700.
Contrast floor: every label's text against its own fill is at least 4.5:1, and every fill against the white sheet is distinguishable at 3:1. Screen brightness is never assumed.

6 · Sending and signal
Amber is only for "saved here, not sent yet". It never means a medical problem. The header band on every worker screen carries three facts in this order: signal, how many updates are waiting, when the last one went.
No signal

Connected

Last sent 2 minutes ago
Waiting to send
Row-level mark. Sits after the state label on any referral holding an unsent change.
